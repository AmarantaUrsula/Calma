Open Road Travel Fund

Open Road Travel Fund (a fictional demo fund) helps people from Texas and Oklahoma who must travel for abortion care.

We don't pay for the procedure. We help with practical costs: gas, bus fare, a hotel, food, and childcare while you travel.

We cover 20 cents per mile round trip, $90 per night for lodging, $25 per day for meals, and $40 per day for childcare, up to $400 in total.

There is no income limit.

This page is made up for the Calma demo.
