Get help paying for your abortion

Desert Bloom Fund (a fictional demo fund) helps people who live in Texas, New Mexico, or Arizona.

Who can get help

  • You live in Texas, New Mexico, or Arizona.
  • Your household income is at or below 300% of the federal poverty level.

How much we give

We pledge a fixed amount of $250 toward your procedure, paid directly to your clinic.

Your clinic will contact us for you. You do not need to call us.
